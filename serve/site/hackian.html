How agletras works

Agletras runs on a modular, distributed architecture. Each agent operates independently, feeding findings back to Agletras's intelligence core for continuous learning.

  • .01

    Autonomous Discovery

    Agletras begins by mapping your environment in depth, identifying both known and hidden assets through our active reconnaissance.

    • Continuous subdomain enumeration
    • DNS resolution
    • Port Scanning
    • Service fingerprinting and change direction
    • Content enumeration and mapping
  • .02

    Intelligent Exploitation

    Unlike scanners, Agletras doesn’t stop at detection. It autonomously executes benign exploitation routines to confirm vulnerabilities.

    • Logic analysis and reasoning
    • Attach path orchestration
    • Non-destructive exploit generation
  • .03

    Primary Source Verification

    Every confirmed finding is paired with verifiable, technical proof. This includes reproducible payloads, evidence, and contextualized impact information, enabling defenders to reproduce, fix and retest vulnerabilities with complete confidence.

    • Step-by-step reproduction blueprints
    • Strategic remediation guidance
